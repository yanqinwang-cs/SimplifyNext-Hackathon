# Device Examination Report

## Source
University IT Forensics Unit.

## Examination Item
Pair of dark-framed glasses submitted for examination after the assessment.

## Item Identifier
DF-26-091

## Examination Summary
The item is a pair of prescription-capable smart glasses marketed under the model name **OptiView Link S2**.

The glasses contain:
- a forward-facing camera;
- two microphones;
- bone-conduction audio output;
- Bluetooth connectivity;
- Wi-Fi connectivity when paired through a companion device;
- limited local event storage;
- a capacitive touch area on the right temple;
- a physical wake / interaction control integrated into the right temple.

The device can capture audio, transmit data through a paired companion device, and play received audio privately through the frame.

## Account and Pairing State
At the time of examination:
- the glasses were paired with a mobile device recorded as **CandidateA-Phone**;
- the companion application account was registered under Candidate A's university email address;
- the device clock was synchronized automatically through the paired phone.

## Local Storage
The device retained an activity database containing wake events, microphone-capture events, connection events, and session identifiers.

The examination did not itself determine what information was transmitted during any particular session.
