# Assessment Rules — Tutorial 5 (Week 6)

## Source
Business Law course assessment instructions.

## Assessment
Tutorial 5 — Week 6

## Assessment Conditions
- Duration: **75 minutes**
- Assessment period: **10:00–11:15**
- Students must complete the work individually.
- Students may use the printed tutorial paper and writing materials.
- Students may not communicate with other students during the assessment.
- Students may not obtain assistance from another person during the assessment.
- Internet access is not permitted.
- Mobile phones, smart watches, tablets, laptops, earbuds, and other connected or electronic devices must be switched off and stored away before the assessment begins.
- Ordinary prescription glasses are permitted.
- Students may not use any electronic device to obtain information, communicate externally, record the assessment, or receive assistance.
- Any uncertainty about whether an item is permitted must be raised with an invigilator before the assessment begins.

## Submission
Students must submit the completed answer booklet when instructed at the end of the assessment.
