## Responses

### Question 1
What is consideration?

Consideration is something of value exchanged between the parties for the agreement.

### Question 2
What is an offer?

An offer is when one party clearly proposes terms to another party and is willing to be bound if the other party accepts.

### Question 3
Is an advertisement always an offer?

No. Usually it is an invitation to treat unless the wording clearly shows that the person intends to be bound.

### Question 4
Ali promises to sell a bicycle to Ben for RM300. Ben says he will think about it and later tries to accept after Ali has already sold it to someone else. Does Ben have a contract?

Probably not because Ben did not accept when Ali made the offer. If Ali already sold it before Ben accepted, the agreement was not completed.

### Question 5
Why is intention to create legal relations important?

Because not every promise is meant to become a legal contract. The parties must intend that the agreement can be enforced.

### Question 6
A seller gives the wrong delivery date by mistake. Is this automatically fraud?

No. Fraud needs intention to deceive. If it was a mistake it may be another problem but not necessarily fraud.
