# Student Support Session Note

## Source
Student support counsellor.

## Date
27 Aug 2026

## Student
Candidate A

## Context
Routine academic-support session requested by the student several days before Tutorial 5.

## Session Note
Candidate A reported feeling increasingly anxious about the upcoming Business Law assessment.

The student said that the Week 6 topic on vitiating factors was difficult to follow and that fraud, misrepresentation, coercion, and undue influence were "all starting to blend together." The student was particularly worried about problem questions because they felt they could remember definitions but did not know how to apply them properly.

Candidate A said they had been revising but still felt "lost once the questions become long."

The student also described becoming physically restless when anxious during tests. They said they tend to touch their face, adjust their glasses, tap their pen, and pause for long periods when they are unsure what to write.

Candidate A expressed concern about performing badly and said they were under pressure to keep their grades up. They said they were afraid that another poor assessment result would affect their confidence in the module.

The student did not report any disciplinary issue or discuss any plan to obtain outside assistance during the assessment.

## Counsellor Note
The session focused on test anxiety, study planning, and breaking long problem questions into smaller steps.
