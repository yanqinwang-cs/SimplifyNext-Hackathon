# Module Scope and Syllabus Extract

## Source
Business Law module syllabus.

## Relevant Teaching Period
Weeks 5–7

## Week 6 Topic
**Vitiating Factors and Free Consent**

Students are expected to:
- explain the concept of free consent;
- distinguish coercion, undue influence, fraud, misrepresentation, and mistake;
- distinguish void and voidable contracts;
- identify the relevant Contracts Act 1950 provisions;
- apply the rules to short factual scenarios;
- reach a brief legal conclusion.

## Statutory Provisions Listed for Week 6
- Contracts Act 1950, s.10
- Contracts Act 1950, s.14
- Contracts Act 1950, s.15
- Contracts Act 1950, s.17
- Contracts Act 1950, s.18
- Contracts Act 1950, s.19

## Authorities Listed for Week 6
- **Kesarmal s/o Letchman Das v Valiappa Chettiar (1954)**

## Assessment Expectations
Students are expected to use an issue-rule-application-conclusion structure where appropriate.

Accurate statutory references are rewarded but exact reproduction of statutory language is not required.

Students may draw on earlier contract topics where relevant.

## Scope Note
The following are **not listed in the Week 6 syllabus or assigned materials**:
- **Bisset v Wilkinson [1927] AC 177**
- detailed treatment of the distinction between statements of fact and honestly held opinion
- extended restitution doctrine following rescission

The syllabus does not prohibit students from learning material outside the listed teaching scope.
