## Known Fixture-Wide Interpretation Notes
- The strongest evidential chain is cumulative: room eyewear + device identity/capability + assessment-time device activity + external-service content + session linkage + timing.
- No single early artefact should be treated as dispositive.
- The student-support note creates a legitimate alternative explanation for fidgeting.
- The alternate-glasses claim creates a real attribution issue that must be weighed against the technical chain.
- The Ray branch is intentionally plausible but non-conclusive.
- Minor wording or legal imperfections in synthetic artefacts should be treated as fixture defects if they materially mislead the agent.
